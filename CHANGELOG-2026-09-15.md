# Kazi Additions Build Changelog - 2026-09-15

Changes since the September 11 changelog.

- Updated the mod version to 4.0.0 and built `KaziAdditions-4.0.0.jar`.
- Added the Supa awakening framework with Reality Marble, Projection, Enhancement, and Kanshou & Bakuya support.
- Added Reality Marble modes for Universe of Endless Blades: Mugen no Kensei, Unlimited Blade Works: Infinite Creation of Swords, and Unlimited Lost Works.
- Added Reality Marble terrain replacement and restoration, custom coarse dirt/sand, barrier visuals, weapon displays, gear displays, charge music, and mode-specific sphere textures.
- Added Projection modes including Rho Aias, Caladbolg, Universe of Infinite Blades, and Instant Sword Replication.
- Added Rho Aias shield visuals, pulsing translucent particles, and hold-duration invulnerability behavior.
- Added Enhancement modes including Overedge, Noble Phantasm Reconstruction: Caliburn, and Noble Phantasm Trace: Gae Bolg.
- Added Caliburn and Gae Bolg VFX, sounds, screen shake, charge behavior, and projectile/impact logic.
- Added Kanshou & Bakuya as a paired sword item with custom 3D rendering, offhand display support, HUD adjustments, and Overedge-enhanced visuals.
- Added icons and lang entries for the new Supa awakening abilities and modes.
- Tuned Reality Marble weapon and gear spawning, spacing, counts, rotations, textures, and performance.
- Tuned Reality Marble cooldown scaling so Mugen and Infinite scale from a 30-second minimum to a 300-second maximum.
- Set Rho Aias cooldown to 15 seconds.
- Increased Caliburn, Gae Bolg, and Instant Sword Replication balance values through the Supa tuning pass.
- Added and optimized VFX for Koku, Kama, Zushi, Gasu beam moves, and Mera Dai Entai while preserving local gameplay functionality.
- Fixed dedicated-server crashes caused by client-only paired sword tooltip/render references.
- Fixed Arclight/server method compatibility crashes in Koku VFX and Kama VFX.
- Fixed Maximum Output: Lapse Blue movement restriction lasting longer than intended.
- Fixed the old Mink subrace replacement issue that could leave Mine Mine no Mi notorious NPC spawning with an empty subrace list.
- Audited recent Kama, Koku, and Supa changes for multiplayer/server crash risks.
