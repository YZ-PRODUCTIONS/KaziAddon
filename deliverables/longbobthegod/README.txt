longbobthegod

Editable Blockbench project: longbobthegod.bbmodel
40 low-poly mesh parts, 20 bones, 256x256 embedded texture, 9 animations.

Reference details: brown side-parted hair, round metal glasses, blue/white/gray costume ears,
ear charm, blue-trimmed black paw jacket, collar and paw pendant, gray furry paw gloves,
and a matching fluffy tail. Dark trousers and blue-trimmed shoes complete the unseen lower body.

Animations: idle, run, arrival, melee, hydra, chloro, gumo, fugu, road.
Runtime geometry and atlas are generated by tools/build_longbob.cjs.

Summon: /summon kazimod:longbobthegod ~ ~ ~
Natural spawning: Overworld, configurable 30-60 minutes, one natural longbobthegod at a time.
Boss techniques: Hydra, Chloro Ball, Doku Gumo, Doku Fugu, Venom Road. No Venom Demon.
Only this boss uses the light-green poison palette; player Doku effects are unchanged.
