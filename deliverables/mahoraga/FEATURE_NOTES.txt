MAHORAGA - KAGE AWAKENING

Unlock: awaken Kage Kage no Mi with Awakening Essence, then equip Mahoraga.
Use once to summon, again to dismiss. Cooldown after dismissal/death: 60 seconds.
Dismissal stops combat immediately, then sinks the shadowed guardian into a black portal
over three seconds. Repeated dismissal requests cannot restart the exit animation.
Logout/dimension cleanup remains immediate. Active guardian projectiles are removed on dismissal.
Only one guardian per owner. Owner death, logout, dimension change, fruit loss,
or removing the equipped summon ability dismisses it.

Base health: 700 HP. Model height: approximately 4.6 blocks.
Normal slash: 32 damage. Overhead slam: 48 damage, 8-block radius.
Punch barrage: six 10-damage punches. Aerial takedown: 18 + 36 landing damage.
Thrown block: 38 damage. Air blast: 32 damage. These are pre-Haki base damages.
Invisible, unbreakable Protection IV netherite armor uses vanilla armor/enchantment rules.
Permanent silent full-body Haki uses native MMNM armor/toughness bonuses at max mastery.
All melee and unreflected ranged attacks use internal destruction, Logia bypass,
and the native Haoshoku infusion damage-boost formula, without Haki visual effects.
Block pose halves frontal adaptable damage after armor/other reductions.
34-tick guard cooldown; reactive guard can interrupt recovery but not committed strikes.

ADAPTATION
Each hostile hit restarts the 200-tick quiet timer and interrupts a turning wheel.
After that quiet period the wheel performs a 20-tick, 45-degree turn.
Each completed turn learns ALL distinct remembered attacks, not just the latest hit.
New hits accumulate in a batch and never overwrite earlier hits when restarting the timer.
Each attack receives one +33% step and each distinct category one +15% step per turn.
Repeated hits or several abilities sharing a category do not multiply that turn's gain.
All unfinished adaptations continue together during subsequent quiet periods until immunity.
Pending memory is bounded to the most recent 128 distinct attacks and 32 categories.
Exact attack resistance: 33%, 66%, 99%, 100%.
Element OR physical-type resistance: +15% per turn, reaching 100% on turn seven.
For a hit covered by both, the stronger resistance applies; the two do not add.
Haki nature never becomes a learned category. Special and void damage are exempt.
Learning belongs to that summon and resets when a new Mahoraga is summoned.

COMBAT
Defends owner, scoreboard teammates, MMNM crewmates and owner's tamed entities.
Learns individual opponents' melee pressure, ranged use and retreating behavior.
Uses predictive pursuit, reactive blocks, collision-checked lateral dodges,
MMNM Geppo, aerial takedowns, block throws and air blasts.
Returns toward the owner before reaching the hard 50-block leash.
If displaced past the leash, moves to safe nearby ground or dismisses if blocked.
Block removal respects mobGriefing, Forge break cancellation and tile entities.

MODEL
Source: C:/blockbench/mahoraga.bbmodel. Original source file was not edited.
All 420 optimized cubes and embedded texture preserved. Sword/head-wing transforms unchanged.
Resting fingers are curled into fists; two thumb joints complete the closed hand pose.
Two animation-only joints control abdomen/cheek inflation; no new cubes.
Monotone motion curves, staggered body follow-through, short lunges and blended transitions.
Run uses an upright support leg, folded heel recovery, opposing arm swings and mild hip bounce.
Walk/run blending shares one continuous footfall phase while retaining each gait's cadence.
Overhead slam extends and strikes with the right blade arm only; the left arm counterbalances.
The downstroke still lands at tick 24; damage, range and cooldowns are unchanged.
17 animation clips in mahoraga_animated.bbmodel, including reverse-summon dismissal.
Portal, sword arcs, impact rings and air pressure trails use small batched meshes.
