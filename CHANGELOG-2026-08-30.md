# Kazi Additions Build Changelog — 2026-08-30

## Abilities and balance

- Goro now receives Kazi-owned copies of Vari, Volt Amaru, Volt Amaru Flight, and Raigo, including their original names, translations, and icons.
- Shinzo Massage and Voltage Up remain disabled on Goro.
- Hollow Purple Nuke now instantly damages everyone in its radius, including its caster, for 50 total damage.
- Forming Hollow Purple Nuke puts Red and Blue startup moves on a 90-second cooldown.
- A Hollow Purple Nuke cannot be formed for 120 seconds after another nuke activates.
- Dismantle single slash now deals 10% of the caster's maximum health.
- Dismantle's five-hit barrage now deals 2% of the caster's maximum health per hit.
- Malevolent Shrine puts all other Kama moves on cooldown for half of Shrine's configured cooldown.
- Unlimited Void gives Koku's other moves the same half-domain-cooldown behavior.
- Fists of Love Barrage's movement boost is now 0 and its step-height boost was removed.
- Kirin's Sleepy and Disabled Abilities effects now last 3 seconds.
- Divine Departure is now a Kazi-owned ability and projectile copy assigned to Saber.
- Divine Departure projectile direct damage was reduced from 100 to 50; its 20-damage impact explosion and 60-damage dash remain unchanged.

## Fixes

- Fixed non-Gomu awakenings incorrectly receiving Gear Fifth, Dawn Whip, Gigant, and Kaminari.
- Gear Fifth abilities now explicitly require an awakened Gomu Gomu no Mi.
- Awakening grants now validate that unlocked fruit abilities belong to the player's current fruit.
- Disabled the Kazi Yami rework replacements so the standard Yami moves remain active.
